Account & Data Deletion Request

Information on how to request the deletion of your account and associated game data for MindSpark: Daily Brain Challenge (Package: com.gujjar.mindspark).

⚠️
Important Warning: Requesting account deletion will schedule the permanent deletion of your profile, all game progress, coin balances, arena records, achievements, and statistics. Once processed, this action cannot be undone.

1. 30-Day Account Deletion Procedure

MindSpark implements a secure 30-day account deletion procedure designed to safeguard users:

  • When an account deletion request is submitted, your account status is immediately changed to pending deletion.
  • You will be logged out immediately across all devices.
  • During this 30-day window, sign-ins with that Google account will display an informative message indicating that account deletion is pending.
  • The administrator reviews and permanently removes your account, personal data, and game records from Firebase Authentication and Firestore within 30 days.
  • Once permanent deletion is completed, you may freely create a brand new account using the same Gmail address if you choose to return in the future.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

Method 1: In-App (Recommended & Instant)

You can initiate deletion directly from within the MindSpark app:

  1. Open the MindSpark app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) from the top-right corner of the Home screen.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and select Delete Account & Data.
  4. Read the confirmation warning detailing data loss and the 30-day procedure.
  5. Tap Agree & Request Delete to confirm.

Your request will be recorded immediately in Firebase, and you will be safely logged out.

Method 2: Web / Email Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it:

  1. Send an email to sajawal0005@gmail.com from the Gmail address associated with your MindSpark account.
  2. Use the Subject line: MindSpark Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your Google account email and optional player username.

Our administrative team will process your request and schedule deletion within 30 days.

3. Types of Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon permanent deletion, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Authentication Profile: Firebase UID, Google display name, email address, profile avatar.
  • Game Progress & State: Total XP, IQ score, brain level, puzzle mastery levels, streaks.
  • Economy & Inventory: Spark coins, arena tokens, purchased avatars, frames, power-ups.
  • Game Records: Daily challenge history, puzzle completion times, high scores, arena wins/losses.
  • Leaderboard Entries: Arena leaderboard scores and global ranking entries.

4. Data Retention Period

Your deletion request is queued for up to 30 calendar days. After this grace period, all data is permanently and irrevocably wiped from Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication. No backups of your personal data are retained.
